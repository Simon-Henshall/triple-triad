How to Play

  • Select 5 cards from your deck to form your hand.
  • Place cards on the 3×3 board one at a time.
  • Compare numbers on adjacent sides — higher number wins and claims the opponent's card.
  • Whoever owns the most cards when the board is full wins.

Controls

↑↓←→ Navigate / Move cursor
Enter Confirm / Select / Place card
Backspace / Esc Cancel / Go back / Deselect card

Special Rules

Elemental
Cards gain +1 on sides matching their element. Five elements form a rock-paper-scissors cycle.
Same
When two sides of your placed card match the touching sides of two adjacent opponent cards, you claim them.
Same Wall
Board edges count as rank A (value 10) for the Same rule.
Plus
When the sum of your placed card's side + the opponent's touching side is equal for two pairs, you claim both.
Combo
Cards captured by Same/Plus trigger a chain reaction — they immediately try to flip further adjacent cards.
Open
Both players see the AI's hand face-up.
Random
Your hand is chosen randomly from your deck instead of letting you pick.
